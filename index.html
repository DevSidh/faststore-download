FastStore customer app

Your store, from browsing to delivery

FastStore is the customer shopping app for Ram Prasad General Store in Janakpur. Explore the customer features below; store staff use a separate billing and management area.

⌕

Find products quickly

Search and browse the store catalogue to find everyday items without scrolling through everything.

🥤

Explore the drinks menu

Open the dedicated drinks section and discover featured beverages and other drink options.

📦

Browse wholesale offers

See wholesale products and their available pricing and purchase details in one place.

🏷️

Use available coupons

Review eligible offers in the coupon area and apply a coupon to your cart before checkout.

🛒

Review your cart

Check selected items and quantities, see your order total, and continue to checkout.

🛵

Follow your orders

View your orders and their latest status, and open order details when you need them.

🔔

Stay up to date

See account notifications and store updates from inside the app.

🎁

Build your shopping streak

Keep track of buying-day streak activity and see the rewards available in your account.

🧾

Keep order details handy

Review order information and download an invoice for a completed order when available.

📍

Manage delivery addresses

Save and update your addresses for a smoother checkout. Delivery options depend on the store’s service area.

💳

Choose checkout payment options

Review the payment methods presented at checkout and choose an available option for your order.

🌙

Personalize your app

Adjust appearance and language preferences in the customer account settings.

⭐

Rate purchased products

Share feedback and ratings for eligible products after purchase.

🔐

Manage your account

Register or sign in, update your profile, manage notifications, and access account and privacy options.
